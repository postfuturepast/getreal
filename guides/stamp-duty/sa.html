Stamp duty in South Australia — 2026 rates

Rates calculated from RevenueSA data, stored in GetReal's Supabase database. Updated when official rates change. Last built: 2026-08-07.

Stamp duty in South Australia uses a progressive bracket system. Unlike every other state, SA offers no first home buyer stamp duty exemption or concession. Eligible first home buyers purchasing a new build may receive the $15,000 First Home Owner Grant instead. On an $800,000 property, a buyer pays approximately $38,730.

Key points

  • South Australia is the only state with no FHB stamp duty concession.
  • Eligible first home buyers purchasing new builds may receive the $15,000 First Home Owner Grant.
  • SA rates are on the higher end at upper price points.
  • No separate owner-occupier vs investor rate.

Frequently asked questions

Is there stamp duty relief for first home buyers in SA?

No stamp duty exemption or concession. This is unique among Australian states. However, eligible first home buyers purchasing a new build may receive the $15,000 First Home Owner Grant.

When is stamp duty due in SA?

Due at or before settlement. Your conveyancer handles this.

Does SA have different rates for investors?

No — the same rates apply regardless of how you intend to use the property.
