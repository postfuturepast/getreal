Stamp duty in Tasmania — 2026 rates

Rates calculated from State Revenue Office Tasmania data, stored in GetReal's Supabase database. Updated when official rates change. Last built: 2026-08-07.

Stamp duty in Tasmania — called duty on conveyances — uses a progressive bracket system. First home buyers purchasing established homes receive a 50% duty concession. Tasmanian property prices are generally lower than mainland capitals, making duty bills smaller in absolute terms. On a $600,000 property, a standard buyer pays approximately $18,247.

Key points

  • First home buyers: 50% duty concession on established properties.
  • New builds may attract different concession arrangements.
  • Tasmanian property prices are generally lower than mainland capitals.

Frequently asked questions

What concessions do first home buyers get in Tasmania?

A 50% concession on stamp duty for established property purchases. The property must be your principal place of residence.

When is stamp duty due in Tasmania?

Within 3 months of the date of the instrument (usually settlement).

Are there duty concessions for new builds in Tasmania?

Separate concession arrangements may apply for new builds — check the State Revenue Office for current conditions.
