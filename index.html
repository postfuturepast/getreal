In the works
Paying it off?

This tool is being built.

The idea is to track your property value and your mortgage together, and flag when something has changed enough that you should act.

It might be:

> Rates have dropped broadly and your bank hasn't passed it on

> Your loan-to-value ratio has improved and you're eligible for a better tier

> Your bank is offering new customers a better deal than you're on

The goal is simple. Keep the bastards honest.

An idea
Selling your investment property?

The CGT rules are changing. This tool would show you exactly what that means in dollars.

From 1 July 2027, the 50% CGT discount on investment properties is gone. If you're holding an investment property and thinking about selling — before or after that date — the difference could be significant.

The idea is to enter:

> When you bought, and what you paid

> Whether you ever lived there (and for how long)

> What you think you could sell for

And get a side-by-side: what you'd owe under the old rules vs the new ones. No surprises at settlement.
